About Me

I am currently a Researcher at Idiap Research Institute in Martigny, Switzerland, working in the area of Secure Biometrics.


Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at the Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Kharagpur. My research interests span Computer Vision, Biometrics, Deep Learning, and Embedded System Design.
